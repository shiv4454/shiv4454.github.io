About

Ops by trade. Automation by instinct.

I work in IT infrastructure — the layer everything else depends on. Servers, networks, operating systems, users, and the thousand small failures that quietly cost a team a day. That work taught me to be systematic, to document properly, and to treat reliability as a feature rather than luck.

Then I noticed the pattern: most of what I was doing by hand was a rule pretending to be a job. Automating it freed up time for the work that actually needed a human — design, debugging, judgement.

Now I take that instinct into security, cloud and AI-driven infrastructure. Not a pivot away from what I know, but the next layer on top of it: hardened systems, automated defence, and AI agents that carry out operational tasks end to end.

01 Automate the repeatable, not the interesting. If a task has a rule, a script should own it.
02 Design for the failure. Redundancy, rollback and a plan B before anything goes live.
03 Security is part of the build, not a review that happens afterwards.
04 Document as you go. The next operator — or future me — should never guess.

Lab & Build in Progress

A personal AI automation lab for IT operations. The goal is simple to state and hard to do: give an operator a single place where AI can actually do the work — monitor systems, diagnose issues, and carry out routine operational tasks through chat, with a human approving anything risky. Built as a working lab rather than a demo, using OpenCode as the agent runtime and Telegram as the operator interface.

Natural-Language Ops

Chat with your infrastructure. Ask for status, request a check, or trigger a runbook — in plain language, from Telegram.

Guarded Execution

Agents propose and act within defined boundaries, with confirmation gates before anything destructive runs.

Operational Insight

Logs, health and history in one view, summarised on demand instead of exported and read by hand.

Automation Engine
